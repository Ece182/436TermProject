Which Websites Can I Trust?

In today’s world, information is everywhere—but knowing how to find, evaluate, and use it effectively is a skill. At Aldeas, we’re helping middle schoolers develop core research skills that will set them up for success in academics and beyond.

1. Finding Reliable Information

Strong research skills empower students to think critically, avoid misinformation, and build confidence in their learning. Learn to ask the right questions, analyze sources, and form their own opinions.

  1. Use trusted sources like educational websites, libraries, and databases.
  2. Identify fake news by checking the author, date, and source credibility.
  3. Compare multiple sources to ensure accuracy.
2. Understanding and Using Information Ethically

Strong research skills empower students to think critically, avoid misinformation, and build confidence in their learning. Learn to ask the right questions, analyze sources, and form their own opinions.

  1. Learn how to paraphrase and cite sources properly.
  2. Avoid plagiarism by giving credit to original authors.
  3. Develop original ideas instead of copying information.
