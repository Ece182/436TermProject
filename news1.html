AI Basics
Master ChatGPT by learning prompt engineering.
Aideas
7 min read – Mar 17, 2025

It’s essential that students learn to think critically about the news and media they encounter every day. Students will demonstrate the ability to identify, evaluate, and use information effectively, find credible and trustworthy sources, and give proper credit.

In this course, students will learn how to use ChatGPT effectively by mastering prompt engineering. They will explore the basics of AI, understand how to interact with AI models, and develop skills to create effective prompts that yield the best results.

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

  • Understand the fundamentals of AI and its applications
  • Master prompt engineering techniques to interact with ChatGPT
  • Evaluate the responses generated by AI models
  • Apply AI tools in various academic and research contexts
  • Recognize the limitations and ethical considerations of using AI

This course is designed for students who want to enhance their research skills and leverage AI technology to improve their academic performance. Whether you are a beginner or have some experience with AI, this course will provide you with the knowledge and skills needed to effectively use ChatGPT.

Join us in this exciting journey to master AI and unlock new possibilities in your research and academic endeavors!
