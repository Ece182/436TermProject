Cite it Right!
Learn proper citation techniques.
Aideas
5 min read – Mar 21, 2025

Proper citation is key to academic integrity. This course teaches students how to cite sources correctly using various styles like APA and MLA.

Students will explore the importance of avoiding plagiarism, understand citation formats, and practice creating bibliographies. These skills are vital for scholarly work.

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

  • Understand different citation styles (APA, MLA)
  • Create accurate in-text citations and references
  • Avoid plagiarism through proper attribution
  • Format bibliographies and reference lists
  • Apply citation skills in academic writing

This course is perfect for students aiming to improve their academic writing and research credibility.

Master the art of citation with this essential course!
